RecipeGuru

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026. Android package: com.recipeextract.app

RecipeGuru (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a recipe clipping and cooking app. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the RecipeGuru Android app, the RecipeGuru website, and related RecipeGuru services.

Information we handle

  • Saved recipes, weekly meals, and grocery lists. Titles, ingredients, steps, source links, meal categories, and grocery items stay on your device (or in this browser if you use the website). They are not stored in a shared RecipeGuru account.
  • Recipe extraction. When you paste a recipe URL, share a link into the app, or paste caption text, that URL or text is sent to the RecipeGuru extract service so we can return a title, ingredients, and steps.
  • Backups you export. If you export a backup from Help, that file stays wherever you save or send it. RecipeGuru does not receive a copy unless you choose to share it with us.
  • No account. RecipeGuru does not require a login, email address, or user profile.

How extraction works

The extract service fetches the page or social caption you asked for and parses a recipe from it. For some social captions, the service may send the caption text to Google Gemini so the ingredients and steps can be cleaned up. We use that information only to return a recipe to you.

The extract service is hosted on Railway. We rate-limit clipping so the service stays available during testing. The source website or social platform you asked us to fetch, and Google if Gemini is used, may also process the URL or caption under their own policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use your recipes or grocery lists for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not require location, contacts, camera, or microphone access for the core app.
  • We do not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK in the current Android app.

Your choices

  • Delete individual saved recipes or grocery items in the app.
  • Clear your grocery list in the app.
  • Export a backup from Help, then uninstall to remove on-device RecipeGuru data.

If your device backs up app data (for example through Google backup), copies of your local RecipeGuru data may be included in those backups according to your device settings.

Children

RecipeGuru is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a failed extract during testing, can be sent through GitHub issues or by using Help → Report a problem in the Android app.
