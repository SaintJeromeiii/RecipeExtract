Pluck

Closed test notes

Use the Android app, not this website, for testing. Recipes you save in the app stay on your phone. Uninstalling deletes them unless you export a backup from Help.

Join the closed test Google Group: recipeguru-testers (recipeguru-testers@googlegroups.com). Play Console closed testing should use this group as the tester list.

10-minute path

  1. Open Pluck. Read the short Help sheet, then dismiss it.
  2. Tap Try a sample recipe. Save it, add it to This week, open Grocery, then Start cooking.
  3. Paste a recipe blog URL (not a homepage). Extract. Check ingredients and steps.
  4. Paste a public TikTok or Instagram recipe URL. If it fails, switch to Caption text and paste the caption.
  5. From Instagram/TikTok, try Share → Pluck. If the link does not land, copy the link, open Pluck, and accept the copied-link prompt.
  6. Export a backup from Help, then Report a problem if anything failed. Include the exact URL and a screenshot.

What “working” looks like

  • A blog recipe comes back with a title, ingredients, and steps in under 15 seconds.
  • A social post either extracts, or clearly tells you to paste the caption — it should not show an empty “Social recipe.”
  • Weekly meals add grocery items. Checking items in Grocery sticks after you close the sheet.

Please do not

  • Hammer Extract on the same Reel over and over. There is an hourly clip limit.
  • Use the Railway website as your kitchen. That is a demo page; your phone is the product.
  • Send private/login-wall recipes. Public posts and public blogs only.
