Full description:
Pluck turns a recipe link or social caption into a clean recipe you can cook from.

Paste a blog URL, TikTok, or Instagram caption. Pluck keeps the title, ingredients, and steps — not the blog filler. Save recipes on your phone, add them to this week, and build a grocery list by aisle.

• Clip from a URL or pasted caption
• Share a link into Pluck from other apps
• Save a private recipe library on your device
• Plan weekly meals and auto-add grocery items
• Cook mode with step timers and a stay-awake screen
• Scale servings, edit, print PDF, and export a backup

Social videos are often blocked by the platform. If a Reel will not extract, copy the caption (ingredients and steps) and paste it in Caption text.

No account required. Recipes stay on your device. See the privacy policy for how extraction works.

Play Console setup for wide testing:
1. Create the app if needed (name Pluck, default language English).
2. Set privacy policy URL above.
3. App content: not for children, no ads, no in-app purchases for this test build.
4. Data safety: no collected user data stored on our servers except URLs/captions sent to extract a recipe. No sharing for advertising.
5. Release → Testing → Internal testing (up to 100 Google accounts). Upload the AAB. Add testers. Share the opt-in link.
6. Closed testing: add the Google Group recipeguru-testers@googlegroups.com as testers, then promote the same AAB. That is the wider track before production.
